This spreadsheet calculates the beat rates of coinciding partials between any two notes in 12-tone equal temperament, for tuning by ear. Pick two notes (A0–C8) and it lists the partials (1–8) that land on the same note. It then gives each pair's beat rate in Hz (|f₂ − f₁|) and how far apart the two partials are in cents. Pairs further apart than an adjustable tolerance are shaded orange, since they may not sound like a clear beat. The lowest coinciding pair for intervals tuners commonly use (thirds, fourths, fifths, sixths and the octave) is shown in bold. That list is editable. Partials are treated as ideal harmonics (n × the fundamental), with no inharmonicity, so real piano strings will differ slightly, especially in the bass and treble. It works in both Excel (.xlsx) and LibreOffice (.ods), and the A4 reference pitch is adjustable (default 440 Hz).
